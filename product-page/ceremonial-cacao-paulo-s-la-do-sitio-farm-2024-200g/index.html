Taste Profile

AGL8 from Paulo's La do Sitio presents a truly transformative taste experience. The flavor journey begins with bold smoky and tobaccoy notes that gradually unfold into a lush green floral profile—a remarkable evolution that reveals the complexity of Amazon rainforest cacao.

The initial impression is rich and earthy, with pronounced smoky and tobacco characteristics that speak to the terroir of the Amazon. As the cacao develops on the palate, these bold notes gracefully transition, opening up to reveal vibrant green and floral flavors that dance across the tongue. This transformation creates a dynamic, multi-layered tasting experience that showcases the unique character of award-winning regenerative cacao from the heart of the Amazon.

Perfect for those seeking a complex, evolving flavor profile that transforms from bold earthiness to delicate floral notes.
