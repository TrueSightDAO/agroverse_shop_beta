Taste Profile

AGL2 from Fazenda Santa Ana presents a mild and pleasantly smooth taste profile that reflects the gentle character of Bahia's regenerative cacao. This cacao offers a delicate floral jasmine note that dances on the palate, complemented by subtle caramel sweetness and an exceptionally creamy, fatty texture that creates a luxurious mouthfeel.

The flavor profile is characterized by its mild nature—approachable and gentle, making it perfect for those new to ceremonial cacao or seeking a smooth, contemplative experience. The jasmine floral notes provide an elegant aromatic quality, while the caramel adds a warm, comforting sweetness. The creamy, fatty texture is particularly notable—this cacao is rich in cocoa butter, creating a velvety smoothness that coats the tongue and lingers beautifully. This is a cacao that invites slow, mindful tasting, allowing its subtle layers to reveal themselves gradually.

Perfect for those seeking a mild, smooth cacao experience with elegant floral notes and exceptional creamy texture.
