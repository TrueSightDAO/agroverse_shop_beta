81% Organic Dark Chocolate Bar (50g) — Fazenda Santa Ana, Bahia Brazil, 2023

This is a single-estate 81% dark chocolate bar (50g) made with 81% cacao and 19% cane sugar, crafted by Kirsten Ritschel (KiKi's Cocoa) from cacao tied to Fazenda Santa Ana in Bahia, Brazil (Coopercabruca network).

Traceability & impact. Each bar carries a unique QR code. Each bar sold plants a tree through Agroverse's tree-planting program.
