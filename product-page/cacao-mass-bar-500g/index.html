Taste Profile

Cacao from Oscar's farm presents a distinctive deep, dark European chocolate taste profile that speaks to the heritage of 80-year-old Criolla trees. This cacao is exceptionally buttery — so rich and smooth that it wraps around your tongue, creating a luxurious, velvety mouthfeel that lingers long after the first taste.

The flavor is characterised by intense chocolate notes with subtle undertones of earth and wood, reflecting the mature trees and regenerative farming practices. The buttery texture is complemented by a smooth, low-acidity finish that makes this cacao ideal for both ceremonial use and premium chocolate making. This is a cacao that demands to be savoured slowly, allowing each layer of flavour to unfold on the palate.

Perfect for those seeking a traditional, rich chocolate experience with exceptional mouthfeel and depth.

How to Use a 500g Cacao Mass Bar

  • Ceremonial brew: shave or grate 25–40g into hot water, whisk until frothy, sweeten to taste.
  • Drinking chocolate: melt into steamed milk with a little sugar and cinnamon.
  • Home chocolate making: melt with cocoa butter and sugar for bars, truffles, or bark.
  • Baking: chop into chunks for brownies, cakes, and cookies.
  • Storage: cool, dry, away from strong odours. The bar keeps for many months; a light bloom is harmless.
