How to Use a 500g Cacao Mass Bar

  • Ceremonial brew: shave or grate 25\u201340g into hot water, whisk until frothy, sweeten to taste.
  • Drinking chocolate: melt into steamed milk with a little sugar and cinnamon.
  • Home chocolate making: melt with cocoa butter and sugar for bars, truffles, or bark.
  • Baking: chop into chunks for brownies, cakes, and cookies.
  • Storage: cool, dry, away from strong odours. The bar keeps for many months; a light bloom is harmless.
