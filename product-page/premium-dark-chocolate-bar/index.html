A monthly journey through our farms

Every month, you receive a shipment of single-estate 81% dark chocolate bars from a different Bahia farm. One month it might be Oscar's century-old grove; the next, Fazenda Santa Ana's regenerative cooperative. You never know which farm until you open the package — and scan the QR code on the back.

That QR code reveals everything: the exact farm, the harvest vintage, the shipment documentation, and the on-chain ledger entry. Each bar is fully traceable from tree to wrapper.

It's a monthly discovery — a curated journey through the best single-estate cacao Brazil has to offer.

Each bar is 81% cacao, 19% cane sugar, 50g, crafted by Kirsten Ritschel (KiKi's Cocoa). No additives, no soy lecithin, no dairy — just cacao and sugar, single-estate, small-batch.

Traceability & impact. Every bar carries a unique QR code linking to its farm, vintage, and on-chain provenance. Every bar sold plants a tree through Agroverse's reforestation program.
