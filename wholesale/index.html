What you'd be putting on the shelf

Stand-up matte pouch, resealable, food-safe. The single-estate origin (e.g. Oscar's Farm, Bahia) is on the front; the QR code lives on the back so a customer in your store can scan, see the farm, and register for tree-planting updates before they reach checkout.

Why it sells in your shop

Every 200g bag carries a unique QR code. When your customer scans it, they get the full provenance of the cacao they're holding:

The exact farm

Single-estate — not a regional blend. They see the named farmer (Oscar, Paulo, Vivi) and the farm's history.

The location, on a live map

The interactive map shows the farm in Bahia or Pará, and the GPS coordinates of the cacao trees.

The story behind the farm

Three generations of cacao wisdom, regenerative agroforestry, photos and short films of the farm in action.

Lab reports — heavy metals verified absent

Every shipment is tested. Reports are linked from each AGL shipment page and the QR scan flow.

A tree planted, in their name

Every bag plants a tree in the Amazon. Customers who register their email get the GPS location of their tree, plus annual satellite imagery showing how the patch of formerly deforested rainforest is recovering.
