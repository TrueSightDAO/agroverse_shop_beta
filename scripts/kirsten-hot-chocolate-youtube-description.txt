Kirsten Ritschel (KiKi's Cocoa, San Francisco) makes hot chocolate with cacao from Agroverse single-estate lots—traceable from farm to bar.

In this vertical short (~43s), Kirsten shows how she turns our craft chocolate into a rich drinking chocolate at home. The bars featured in our shop are made by KiKi's Cocoa using cacao from regenerative farms in Brazil.
