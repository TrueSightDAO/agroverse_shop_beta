The Farm Story

Fazenda Santa Ana is a farm with deep roots in Bahia, Brazil, known for its rich history and vibrant community traditions. The farm is part of the Coopercabruca cooperative, which supports small-scale farmers through regenerative practices and sustainable cacao cultivation.

The farm's commitment to quality and community is reflected in its participation in cooperative initiatives that benefit both farmers and the local ecosystem. Through Coopercabruca, Fazenda Santa Ana contributes to a network of regenerative cacao producers working together to preserve traditional farming methods while ensuring sustainable livelihoods.

Fazenda Santa Ana represents the collaborative spirit of Bahia's cacao farming community, where individual farms work together through cooperatives to maintain quality standards, share knowledge, and support each other in producing exceptional cacao while preserving the region's agricultural heritage.

Chocolate Morbeck is the craft-chocolate brand produced right on Fazenda Santa Ana—turning the farm’s own cacao into small-batch bars and confections so visitors and customers can taste the estate in finished chocolate, not just in beans. For the latest releases, farm moments, and behind-the-scenes craft, follow @chocolate.morbeck on Instagram.

Chocolate Morbeck — Craft Chocolate on Fazenda Santa Ana

Chocolate Morbeck is made at Fazenda Santa Ana, using cacao grown on the same land. That short path from tree to tempering is what many people call tree-to-bar or farm-to-bar: the same soil, varieties, and harvest rhythms you’ll read about in our farm story show up in the bar you unwrap.

Because the brand lives on Instagram, you’ll find the most current seasonal products, pop-ups, and day-in-the-life snapshots there—far more up to date than any static page. We link to it here so Agroverse visitors exploring Santa Ana can connect directly with the people making chocolate on the ground.

Agroverse partners with Santa Ana for traceable ceremonial cacao and beans; Morbeck is the farm’s own label for finished chocolate—complementary stories from the same terroir.
