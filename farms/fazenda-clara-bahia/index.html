The Farm Story

Fazenda Clara is the cabruca cacao farm of Fernando Soller Gimenez and his wife Clara, in the rural cacao country around Itacaré on Brazil's southern Bahia coast. Black King visited on 10 September 2024 — a day that started in the cabruca shade and ended with cacao being tasted straight off the tree.

The trees carry a flavour that Fernando insists on sharing with everyone who visits. “It has something of melon, for sure” — chefs use this cacao like the secret spice of a recipe, and nearly a hectare of the farm is planted to it. Tasting it on the land, the reaction is always the same: “Ele é muito delicioso!”

Fernando explains the farming with visible pride: a dilute mix of seawater minerals — which carry every mineral that exists — fed to the trees alongside nitrogen, pushing the cacao to produce more of what makes it special. The farm was site-inspected under Black King's FSVP programme on 10 September 2024 (cacao trees; inspection record on file).
