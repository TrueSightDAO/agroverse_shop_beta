About The Alchemist

The Alchemist, run by Sammi Whitchurch, is a pop-up vendor that brings the transformative power of ceremonial cacao to gatherings across the nomadic festival circuit. From Skooliepalooza in the Arizona desert to other seasonal gatherings, The Alchemist creates spaces for nourishment, connection, and intentional community.

At Skooliepalooza, one of the most vibrant gatherings on the nomadic circuit, The Alchemist joins fellow travelers and seekers in a celebration of life on the road. Here, Sammi crafts ceremonial cacao drinks that serve as both affordable nourishment and community medicine—warm cups of connection shared around campfires, under starlit skies, among kindred spirits.

What makes The Alchemist unique is Sammi's personal journey with cacao. As someone who understands the body's innate wisdom, she approaches each serving as a ritual of trust and nourishment. Her practice embodies the belief that when we listen to our bodies and honor what they need, transformation follows naturally.

As an Agroverse partner, The Alchemist shares regenerative Amazonian cacao with communities throughout the seasonal festival circuit, supporting both conscious consumption and rainforest restoration. Each cup becomes an opportunity to connect with the land, with community, and with practices that honor both tradition and regeneration.

To find The Alchemist at upcoming gatherings, follow Sammi on Instagram for current locations and event schedules. She's part of the vibrant community that follows the nomadic festival circuit—bringing warmth, nourishment, and connection wherever the road leads.

"It's time to understand your body knows exactly what your body needs, trust." — Sammi Whitchurch, The Alchemist
