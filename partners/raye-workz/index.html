About Raye Workz

Raye Workz, run by Ranette, is a pop-up vendor that brings handcrafted items and regenerative cacao to gatherings throughout the Pacific West Coast journey. From the spring gathering at the Okanogan Family Barter Faire in Washington to winter desert gatherings in Arizona and California, Raye Workz creates spaces for connection, celebration, and conscious commerce.

At the Okanogan Family Barter Faire, Raye Workz joins other pop-up vendors like Llama Bus and Prism Percussions, offering their unique handcrafted items alongside Agroverse's regenerative ceremonial cacao. This vibrant gathering brings together artisans, farmers, musicians, and families in a spirit of exchange and community building.

During the winter season, Raye Workz migrates with the nomad community to desert gatherings in Arizona Quartzite and California's Salton Sea and Joshua Tree areas. At events like Tribe Boheme, Van Aid, SchooliePalooza, Bombay Beach Binnelie, Boondockers bash, Rubber Tramp Rendezvous, and the Quartzite gem show, you can find Raye Workz offering their handcrafted items alongside regenerative cacao.

As an Agroverse partner, Raye Workz shares regenerative Amazonian cacao with communities throughout the seasonal migration, supporting both conscious consumption and rainforest restoration. Each interaction becomes an opportunity to connect with the land, with community, and with practices that honor both tradition and innovation in sustainable living.

To find Raye Workz at upcoming gatherings, check Ranette's Facebook profile for current locations and event schedules. They're part of the vibrant community that follows the Pacific West Coast journey—from spring gatherings in Washington to winter celebrations in the desert.
