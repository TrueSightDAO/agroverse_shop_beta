About The Whole Body Repair Clinic

The Whole Body Repair Clinic, founded by Leesa Bolden, is a sanctuary for holistic healing that combines traditional Chinese medicine with modern therapeutic approaches. With over three decades of experience in acupuncture and massage therapy, Leesa has created a nurturing space where wellness seekers can experience comprehensive healing for body and mind.

The clinic offers a unique blend of ancient wisdom and contemporary therapies, including acupuncture, therapeutic massage, herbal and nutritional consultations, Qigong instruction, PEMF healing mat therapy, red light therapy, ionic footbath detoxification, infrared detoxification, and micro-needling. This integrated approach supports the body's natural healing processes and promotes balance and harmony in both physical and mental well-being.

As an Agroverse partner, The Whole Body Repair Clinic incorporates regenerative ceremonial cacao into their healing sessions, creating powerful synergies between ancient plant medicine and holistic therapeutic practices.
