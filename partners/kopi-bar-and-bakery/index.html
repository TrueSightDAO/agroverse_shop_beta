About Kopi Bar & Bakery

Kopi Bar & Bakery is the project of Chef Nora Haron, bringing Indonesian-Singaporean flavors to Berkeley with a distinctly Californian sensibility. Located inside the Berkeley Art Museum & Pacific Film Archive (BAMPFA), the café offers sustainably sourced coffee, handmade pastries, and savory dishes inspired by Nora's heritage.

From avocado coffee to calamansi olive oil cakes, sambal mushroom sandwiches, and sourdough built on a 10-year-old starter — every item reflects Nora's deep culinary roots and her years of experience at Tartine and Blue Bottle Coffee. The café is open to all, with or without museum admission.

Kopi Bar & Bakery now carries Agroverse ceremonial cacao — single-estate, QR-traced, and tree-planting — for their community to enjoy.
