The Alchemist at Skooliepalooza: Turning Ceremonial Cacao into Community Medicine

Published: May 15, 2026 By Agroverse

Sammi Whitchurch is The Alchemist—a pop-up vendor who brings ceremonial cacao to Skooliepalooza and the nomadic festival circuit. In this vertical clip (~49s), she shows what a morning of community medicine looks like: perfectly roasted beans, melted into oat milk, served cup by cup to the people gathered around her.
