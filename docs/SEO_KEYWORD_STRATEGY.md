# SEO Keyword Strategy — Agroverse.shop

This strategy is driven by competitor research in **market_research/ceremonial_cacao_seo** (USA ceremonial cacao competitors, full-site index, and positioning analysis). Keep homepage, product pages, and key landing pages aligned with the match + differentiate approach below.

---

## Match (core terms we must show up for)

| Keyword / phrase | Where to use |
|------------------|--------------|
| ceremonial cacao | Homepage title, meta, H1; product pages |
| ceremonial cacao USA | Homepage, meta description |
| buy ceremonial cacao | Product pages, category |
| organic ceremonial cacao | Product pages, meta, product titles |
| single estate ceremonial cacao | Product pages, farm/shipment pages |

---

## Differentiate (unique angles)

| Keyword / phrase | Why us |
|------------------|--------|
| regenerative cacao / regenerative ceremonial cacao | Amazon rainforest regeneration |
| ceremonial cacao traceability / farm traceability | Farms, shipments, harvest years |
| ceremonial cacao Brazil / Amazon cacao | Origin story |
| cacao circles / ceremonial cacao circles | Gatherings, community |
| ceremonial cacao wholesale | B2B + retail |

---

## Content briefs

- **Homepage:** Title and meta must include “ceremonial cacao” and differentiators (regeneration, cacao circles). Meta description ideally 150–160 chars for SERP; include “organic ceremonial cacao” or “ceremonial cacao USA.”
- **Product pages:** Each ceremonial product: title/meta with product name + “ceremonial cacao,” “organic,” and origin. See `PRODUCT_CREATION_CHECKLIST.md` for schema and URLs.
- **Blog / Cacao Journeys:** Use “ceremonial cacao,” “regenerative,” “traceability,” “single origin” where relevant.
- **Farms / Shipments:** Emphasize “single origin,” “farm traceability,” “ceremonial cacao [origin].”

---

## Tone and differentiators to keep

- Regeneration, Amazon rainforest, sustainable/regenerative farming
- Specific farms, shipments (AGL), harvest years
- Taste profiles, flavor notes, origin
- Cacao circles, gatherings, mindful connection
- Retail + wholesale

**Competitor insights (full-site index, 80 pages per competitor):**
- **Ceremonial Cacao (Ora):** 24 product pages, heavy "ceremonial cacao" + origin in titles, "organic" frequent, "regenerative" and "traceability" present.
- **Ma Cacao:** "regenerative" 3x, "ceremonial" 2x, "origin" 5x across 57 pages. Emphasizes "ceremony bar" and origin.
- **Four Visions:** Ceremonial cacao category exists; broader plant medicine focus.
- **Bar and Cocoa:** Multi-brand retailer with "ceremonial cacao" collection; emphasizes "single estate."

**Pattern:** Competitors consistently use "ceremonial cacao" + origin in product titles. "Organic" is standard. Our differentiators (regenerative, traceability, Amazon) align well.
