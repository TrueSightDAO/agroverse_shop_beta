Meet the farmers behind your cup

🌳 Oscar's Ceremonial Cacao

Three generations of cacao wisdom. Oscar's grandfather planted trees 80 years ago that are still producing today. His 100-year-old farm in Bahia features 80-year-old Criolla trees — a testament to regenerative farming that spans generations.

Grown in the shade of the Atlantic Forest, Oscar's cacao is fine-flavored Criollo, yielding 900-1000kg per hectare through regenerative agroforestry that preserves biodiversity.

🍃 Paulo's Cacao Tea

From the heart of the Amazon rainforest in Pará, Paulo and his wife run La do Sitio — an award-winning farm that has won multiple regional cacao awards. Their farm is part of the CEPOTX cooperative, supporting small-scale farmers through regenerative practices.

Each cup of Paulo's cacao tea is a celebration of the Amazon's biodiversity — a reminder that the forest is not just a resource to extract, but a partner to protect.
