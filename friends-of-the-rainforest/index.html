Meet the farmers behind your cup

🌳 Oscar's Ceremonial Cacao

Grown in the shade of the Atlantic Forest in Bahia, Brazil. Oscar has been farming this land for generations, using regenerative practices that restore the soil and protect the watershed. One sip connects you to his family, his land, and the trees that make it possible.

🍃 Paulo's Cacao Tea

From the heart of Pará, Paulo harvests wild cacao from the forest floor. His tea is a celebration of the Amazon's biodiversity — each cup a reminder that the forest is not just a resource to extract, but a partner to protect.
